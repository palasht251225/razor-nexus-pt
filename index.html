Designed and built RazorNexus to bridge sovereign AI agents with payment rails. As consumer agents begin autonomous execution, financial infrastructure must support programmatic negotiation, elasticity-aware discounting, and verifiable delegation boundaries.

Developed a clean, self-contained architecture runnable immediately in modern browser environments without build overhead.
