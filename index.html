💡 Why I Built RazorNexus:

"Today, AI assistants like ChatGPT can write text, but they can't shop or buy things for you. Whenever you want to buy something online, you still have to open multiple tabs, compare bundles, search for coupon codes, and enter card details.

I built RazorNexus to make AI shopping real. It allows your personal AI to talk directly to store systems, bargain for bundle deals, and safely complete payments through Razorpay with strict spending limits so you're always in control."

🤝 1. Automatic Bargaining

Your AI shopper automatically asks the store for bundle discounts (like a free case or warranty) and gets discounts for paying via UPI.

🛡️ 2. Guaranteed Spending Safety

Set daily limits (like ₹10,000/day). The AI can never spend more than you allow, and big purchases always ask for your permission first.

⚡ 3. Instant Razorpay Checkout

Built directly on Razorpay's trusted infrastructure, supporting Instant Turbo UPI QR codes, Cards, and No-Cost EMI options.

💬 4. WhatsApp Cart Recovery

Helps stores automatically re-engage shoppers who left without buying by sending them a limited-time discount link on WhatsApp.
